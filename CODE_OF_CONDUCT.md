# Code of Conduct

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Tideline to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a good community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to open source, to Stellar or to cross-chain tooling
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home address or email, without their permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate action in
response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why
when they do.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any place where
someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately. Open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Maryjane-nmesoma/tideline/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct
report", or message the repository owner through their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps in deciding consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning for unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour, with an explanation. A public
   apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved
   for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from the project's spaces for serious or sustained violations.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of violations, or for harassment of a person or group.
